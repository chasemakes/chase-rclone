Chase Rclone privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Chase Rclone is operated by Chase Bailly for private use with his Google Drive accounts and shared drives he is authorized to access.

Data access and use

Rclone accesses file contents, names, folders, and related metadata only to perform the operator’s file-management and transfer requests. The Google Drive permission allows listing, reading, creating, modifying, and deleting files. Google account identity may be read to confirm the correct account is connected.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials, cached files, upload queues, and operational logs are stored on the operator’s computer. File data is transferred between that computer and the Google Drive destinations selected by the operator. This configuration does not provide a separate public server that collects Drive data, sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Retention and control

Local cached data and credentials remain until managed or removed by the operator. Pending uploads may remain cached until successfully transferred. Access can be revoked through Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not delete files already stored in Google Drive.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: chasebailly@gmail.com.
